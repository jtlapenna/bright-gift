    <h2 class="section-title">How to Choose the Right Graduation Gift</h2>
    <ul class="tips-list">
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Start with their path:</strong> College-bound grads may need dorm essentials; workforce-bound grads might appreciate professional gear or commute helpers.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Think small-space friendly:</strong> Stackable, foldable, and multi-use items win in dorms and apartments. The <a href="https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/plan-for-college/college-life/what-to-bring-to-college" target="_blank" rel="noopener">College Board’s move‑in guidance</a> is handy for planning.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Make it personal:</strong> Add a handwritten card, custom engraving, or a photo. Meaningful touches turn practical gifts into lasting keepsakes.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Bundle for impact:</strong> Pair related items—like a desk lamp + planner + pens—for a cohesive setup that feels premium without overspending.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Leave room for choice:</strong> If you’re unsure, a thoughtful book plus a small gift card lets them pick extras they truly need.</li>
    </ul>
