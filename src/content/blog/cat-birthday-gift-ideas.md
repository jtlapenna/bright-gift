  <div class="how-to-choose">
    <h2 class="section-title">How to Choose the Perfect Cat Birthday Gift</h2>
    <ul class="tips-list">
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Match their play style:</strong> Stalkers love wand toys and tunnels; sprinters enjoy lasers and long hallways; problem-solvers crave puzzle feeders.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Prioritize safety:</strong> Look for sturdy bases, non-toxic materials, and true breakaway buckles on collars.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Think enrichment zones:</strong> Combine a vertical perch, a scratcher, and a cozy bed to cover climb, scratch, and rest.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Size and weight matter:</strong> Check weight limits for perches and harness measurements for a secure fit.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Maintenance:</strong> Favor washable covers, replaceable scratch posts, and easy-to-clean stainless steel where possible.</li>
    </ul>
