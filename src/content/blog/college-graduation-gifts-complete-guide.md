H2: Frequently asked questions
Q: Is cash okay for a college graduation gift?
A: Yes. It’s practical, appreciated, and can be paired with a personal note to make it feel thoughtful. Retail research frequently lists cash and gift cards among the top graduation gifts.

Q: What if I don’t know their plans yet?
A: Choose flexible items—thank-you cards, a power bank, an e-gift card—or wait until they share their next step and then gift accordingly.

Q: Do I bring a gift to the ceremony?
A: Ceremonies are busy, and gifts are easy to misplace. It’s better to bring a card or send your gift to their home or party.

Q: How can I avoid duplicates?
A: Ask a close family member if a list exists. If not, choose items that can be used in multiples (towels, storage, notebooks) or opt for a gift card.

Q: What if the grad is moving far away?
A: Lightweight, packable, or digital gifts are best: e-gift cards, cash, Bluetooth trackers, packing cubes, or an e-reader.
