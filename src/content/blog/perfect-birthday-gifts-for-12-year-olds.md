## How to Choose the Right Gift
- **Consider their Interests:** Think about what activities or subjects they show enthusiasm for.
- **Age Appropriateness:** Ensure the gift matches their maturity level and is safe for their age.
- **Educational Value:** Look for gifts that offer learning opportunities alongside entertainment.
- **Quality and Durability:** Choose items that will withstand the test of time and active use.
- **Parental Guidance:** Some gifts may require setup or supervision; consider the parents' role in this.
