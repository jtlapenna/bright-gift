  <div class="how-to-choose">
    <h2 class="section-title">How to Choose the Right 5-Year-Old Birthday Gift</h2>
    <ul class="tips-list">
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Match interests:</strong> Pick a theme they already adore—dinosaurs, space, pets, or princesses—then choose a toy, book, or puzzle in that world.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Balance active and calm:</strong> Gift one movement toy (scooter or rockets) and one quiet activity (books or puzzles) to fit different moods.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Think durability:</strong> Favor sturdy materials and washable parts so the gift lasts beyond the party glow.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Consider space:</strong> If the family has a small home, opt for compact sets (tiles, books) or foldable gear (tents).</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Plan for sharing:</strong> Games and building sets with plenty of pieces keep the peace during playdates and siblings’ turns.</li>
    </ul>
