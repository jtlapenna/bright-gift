Grandparents Day is a good excuse to make a small moment feel deliberate. A gift does not need to be expensive. The ones grandparents tend to keep are the things that remind them who gave it, what the family has shared, or when they will get to see you next.

In the United States, National Grandparents Day falls on the first Sunday after Labor Day. In 2026, that is <a href="https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/us/national-grandparents-day" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Sunday, September 13</a>. If you are shopping for a grandparent who says they do not need anything, start with a family photo, a story, a comfort they will use, or a plan you can do together.

This list is for gifts from grandchildren of any age. For a larger milestone, see our [80th birthday gift ideas for seniors](/blog/80th-birthday-gift-ideas-seniors/). If your family has just welcomed a baby, our [gifts for new grandparents](/blog/gifts-for-new-grandparents/) has ideas built for that season.

## Quick Picks by Grandparent Type

- **For the sentimental grandparent:** a photo calendar, handwritten letter, or memory box.
- **For the one who loves hosting:** a family recipe journal, conversation cards, or a serving board with a note.
- **For the gardener:** a window bird feeder, garden kneeler, or a child-planted herb kit.
- **For long-distance grandparents:** a digital photo frame, recordable storybook, or a scheduled video-call kit.
- **For kids making the gift:** a framed drawing, decorated picture book, or a coupon for a day together.

## How to Make a Grandparents Day Gift Feel Personal

Use one detail that only belongs to your family. Add a photo from a trip, write down a phrase your grandparent says, include a recipe they taught you, or attach a note about a memory you want to make next. That detail does more work than another decorative object.

Think about daily life, too. A gift is easier to enjoy when it fits a favorite chair, garden routine, coffee break, bookshelf, or phone table. Keep setup simple. If the gift needs batteries, an app, or assembly, set it up before you give it when possible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are good Grandparents Day gifts from grandkids?

The best Grandparents Day gifts from grandkids combine a personal detail with something useful or shareable. Photo gifts, a handwritten letter, a family recipe journal, a game to play together, or a simple comfort gift all work well because they reflect a real relationship.

### When is Grandparents Day in 2026?

National Grandparents Day is Sunday, September 13, 2026. In the United States, it is observed on the first Sunday after Labor Day. The date follows the federal observance described in <a href="https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title36-section125" target="_blank" rel="noopener">36 U.S.C. § 125</a>.

### How much should you spend on a Grandparents Day gift?

There is no required amount. A child-made card or letter can mean as much as a larger gift. For a purchased item, $15 to $50 is enough for a thoughtful photo gift, book, blanket, garden item, or activity set.

### What can a child give a grandparent who has everything?

Choose something that records a family story or creates time together: a drawing in a frame, a photo calendar, a memory journal, a family game, or a planned afternoon with a handwritten invitation.
