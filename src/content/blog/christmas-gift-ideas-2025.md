## How to Choose the Right Gift
- Match the gift to daily routines (commute, workouts, reading, cooking).
- Consider material quality (stainless steel, cotton, cast iron) for longevity.
- Balance budget with use frequency—everyday items often deliver the best value.
- Prefer neutral colors/styles if you’re unsure of their taste.
- If in doubt, pair a small, useful item with a heartfelt note.

## FAQs

### What are good last‑minute Christmas gift ideas?
Digital gifts (ebooks, audiobooks, streaming rentals), same‑day delivery items, and experience vouchers are reliable options when you’re short on time.

### How much should I spend on holiday gifts?
Match your budget to your relationship and frequency of use. Under $25 for casual gifts, $25–$75 for close friends and family, and more for major, long‑term items.

### Are Amazon search links okay for gifts?
Yes—search links surface multiple relevant products and current pricing. For books, direct Bookshop.org links by ISBN are ideal when you have a specific title.

### What if I don’t know their style?
Choose quality basics in neutral colors, or gifts that improve daily routines (tumblers, power banks, cozy throws). Include a gift receipt when possible.
