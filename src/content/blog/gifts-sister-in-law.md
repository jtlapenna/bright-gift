    <h2 class="section-title">How to Choose the Right Gift</h2>
    <ul class="tips-list">
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Listen for hints:</strong> Has she mentioned hobbies, a new job, a trip, or a recent move? Match the gift to that moment.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Consider her style:</strong> Neutral and classic, or bright and bold? Choose colors and materials she already wears.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Think practical + elevated:</strong> Everyday items feel special when you choose higher-quality materials or a personalized detail.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Mind care and materials:</strong> Include care notes for silk, wool, or leather so she can enjoy the gift longer.</li>
