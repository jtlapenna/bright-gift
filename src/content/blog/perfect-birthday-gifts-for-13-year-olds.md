## How to Choose the Perfect Gift
- **Interest Alignment:** Consider what they love doing in their free time.
- **Skill Level:** Opt for gifts that match their development stage.
- **Durability:** Ensure the gift is made to last and withstand regular use.
- **Educational Value:** Gifts that offer learning opportunities are always a plus.
- **Safety:** Always check age-appropriateness and safety certifications.
