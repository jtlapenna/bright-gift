## How to Choose the Right Be Rooted Gift

- **Match the format to the routine:** Daily formats suit heavier schedules, while undated planners work better for flexible or changing routines.
- **Think about where it will live:** A larger planner works for a desk setup; a smaller one is better for backpacks or commuting.
- **Consider how they already plan:** If they use digital calendars, gift a notepad, journal, or accessory set instead of a full planner.
- **Use the cover art as a tiebreaker, not the whole decision:** Design matters, but the format is what determines whether the gift gets used.
- **Build around one hero item:** Start with the main planner or journal, then add tabs, pens, or a desk pad to complete it.
