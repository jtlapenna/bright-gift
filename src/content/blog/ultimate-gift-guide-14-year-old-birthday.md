## How to Choose the Perfect Gift
- **Consider their interests:** Match the gift to their hobbies and passions.
- **Think practical and fun:** Balance entertainment with educational value.
- **Age appropriateness:** Ensure the gift is suitable for their developmental stage.
- **Quality over quantity:** Choose items that offer lasting value.
- **Personalization:** Add a personal touch to make the gift memorable.
