### Why personalized beats generic for kids

Kids are used to stories about other people. When the hero looks like them and the story is clearly about *them*, the connection is different. They're not just listening; they're in the story. That kind of representation matters, and it's why personalized children's books tend to get requested again and again at bedtime. A Little Hero Labs book fits that role without feeling like a one-off novelty. The story and art are made to hold up over many reads. Little Hero Labs leans into that without feeling gimmicky. The story has a real message (inner voice, bravery, self-belief) that parents and grandparents can get behind, while the customization makes it a gift the child will remember.
