  <div class="how-to-choose">
    <h2 class="section-title">How to Choose the Right Cat Birthday Gift</h2>
    <ul class="tips-list">
      <li class="tip-item">Match the gift to your cat’s play style. Chasers love lasers and wands; wrestlers prefer kickers; curious cats enjoy puzzle feeders.</li>
      <li class="tip-item">Prioritize safety and materials. Look for sturdy stitching, secure parts, and easy-to-clean fabrics or stainless steel.</li>
      <li class="tip-item">Think enrichment first. Aim for gifts that encourage natural behaviors—climbing, scratching, foraging, and hunting.</li>
      <li class="tip-item">Keep it manageable. Short, frequent play sessions beat marathon play for most cats, so choose toys that are quick to set up.</li>
      <li class="tip-item">Rotate and refresh. Swap toys weekly and refresh catnip to prevent boredom and extend the life of your gifts.</li>
    </ul>
