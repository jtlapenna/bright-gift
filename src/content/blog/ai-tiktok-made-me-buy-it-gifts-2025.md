## FAQs

### Are “viral” gifts actually good quality?
Yes—if you vet them. Look for recent reviews, warranty info, and materials (stainless steel, OEKO‑TEX textiles). We favored items with consistent performance beyond the initial hype.

### What budget should I plan for TikTok‑famous gifts?
Most winners fall between $15–$100. Bundles (e.g., frother + mugs) make a strong gift under $50 without feeling cheap.

### Are Amazon search links okay or should I link exact products?
Search links are fine for general categories because stock and models change weekly. For books, direct Bookshop.org links to specific titles work best.

### How do I choose colors and sizes when I don’t know their style?
Pick neutral colors (beige, gray, navy) and adjustable or one‑size options. Include a gift receipt to simplify exchanges.

## How to Choose the Right Viral Gift
- Favor items that solve an everyday problem (charging, storage, sleep).
- Check material quality and warranty; skip single‑use gimmicks.
- Read the most recent reviews—viral items update often.
- When unsure, bundle two smaller upgrades (e.g., frother + glass mugs).
