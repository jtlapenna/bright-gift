## How to choose the right sleep tech gift (without guessing)

1. **Start with the bottleneck:** Is it falling asleep, staying asleep, temperature, noise, or wake-up routine?
2. **Pick the lowest-friction fix:** If they won’t wear a tracker, don’t buy a tracker.
3. **Avoid “subscription traps”:** Choose physical products they can use immediately.
4. **Think privacy:** Sleep data can be sensitive—some people prefer non-tracking upgrades.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best sleep tech gifts?

The best sleep tech gifts solve one specific problem (light, noise, temperature, routine). For most people, a sunrise alarm clock, white-noise machine, or cooling upgrade beats complicated tracking.

### Are sleep trackers worth it?

They’re worth it for people who enjoy data and want to experiment (consistency, bedtime, late caffeine). If metrics increase stress, stick to environment upgrades.

### What’s the best gift for someone who can’t sleep?

Go “foundation first”: blackout curtains + white-noise machine + a cooling solution if they run hot. Then consider routines like sunrise alarms.
