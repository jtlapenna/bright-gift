  <div class="how-to-choose">
    <h2 class="section-title">How to Choose the Right Gift for a 10-Year-Old</h2>
    <ul class="tips-list">
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Match their current obsession.</strong> Ask a parent or scan recent drawings and school projects for clues (dinosaurs, trains, art, animals).</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Balance challenge with success.</strong> Look for kits and games that start easy and scale up so kids feel capable right away.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Think durability and re-play.</strong> Sturdy materials, refillable parts, and multiple build options extend the gift’s life.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Encourage independence.</strong> Choose activities they can tackle solo or with a friend—clear instructions are key.</li>
      <li class="tip-item"><strong>Consider space and noise.</strong> If they live in an apartment, puzzles and books may be better than a drum kit.</li>
    </ul>
