### Wooden Name Puzzle

**Why it's great:** A chunky wooden name puzzle helps with letter recognition and fine motor skills while making the child's name a visible, touchable part of their room. Durable and timeless.

**Practical tip:** Choose a puzzle with large, rounded pieces for toddlers; older kids can handle more letters or longer names.

### Custom Name Building Blocks or Train

**Why it's great:** Wooden blocks or train cars printed with each letter of the child's name turn spelling into play. They stack, rearrange, and see their name come together.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What age is best for personalized children's books?

Personalized storybooks work well from around age 1 (as a read-aloud) through age 7. Toddlers and early readers get the biggest thrill seeing their name and likeness in the story; older kids still enjoy them as keepsakes.

### Are personalized gifts for kids worth the price?

Yes. Personalized gifts often cost a bit more than generic ones, but they're used and treasured longer. Kids connect more when they see their name or face in a book or toy, which makes the gift more meaningful and memorable.

### What personalized gifts do kids love most?

Kids especially love personalized storybooks where they're the hero, name puzzles and toys they can touch and play with, and keepsakes like blankets or wall art that make them feel seen and special.
