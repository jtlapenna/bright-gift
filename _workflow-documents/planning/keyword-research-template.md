## Template Usage Instructions

### How to Use This Template
1. **Copy the template** for each new blog post
2. **Fill in all sections** before writing begins
3. **Use keyword research tools** to populate data
4. **Review competitive analysis** to identify opportunities
5. **Follow the content outline** during writing
6. **Check off technical SEO items** before publishing
7. **Monitor performance metrics** after publishing

### Required Tools
- **Keyword Research**: Google Ads Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, SEMrush
- **Competitive Analysis**: Ahrefs, SimilarWeb, Moz
- **SEO Analysis**: Google Search Console, PageSpeed Insights
- **Content Planning**: Google Trends, Answer the Public

### Best Practices
- **Focus on user intent** over search volume alone
- **Prioritize low-competition, high-value keywords**
- **Create comprehensive, helpful content**
- **Include diverse affiliate partners**
- **Monitor and optimize based on performance**
- **Update content regularly** to maintain rankings
