## Cross-Platform Content Strategy

### Key Messaging
1. **Relatability:** "We've all been there" - acknowledge the universal panic
2. **Reassurance:** "Don't panic" - provide calm, helpful solutions
3. **Quality:** Emphasize that emergency gifts can still be thoughtful
4. **Variety:** Show multiple solution types (digital, physical, experiences)
5. **Practicality:** Focus on actionable tips and real solutions

### Content Variations
- **Time-Sensitive Posts:** Emphasize speed and urgency
- **Quality-Focused Posts:** Highlight thoughtful, personal touches
- **Experience Posts:** Focus on memorable, experience-based gifts
- **Budget Posts:** Highlight affordable last-minute options
- **Personal Story Posts:** Share relatable emergency gift scenarios

### Posting Schedule Suggestions
- **Holiday Seasons:** Increase frequency around holidays/birthdays
- **Peak Shopping Days:** Post more on weekends and gift-giving occasions
- **Consistent Engagement:** Post 2-3 times per week with different angles
- **Evergreen Content:** Refresh and repost high-performing content monthly

### Analytics to Track
- Engagement rates per platform
- Click-through rates to blog post
- Comments about personal emergency gift stories
- Shares and saves (especially Pinterest and Instagram)
- Time spent on blog post
- Conversion to actual gift purchases
