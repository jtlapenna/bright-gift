## Cross-Platform Content Strategy

### Key Messaging
1. **Your child is the hero** — Not a generic character; they see themselves in the story.
2. **Keepsake, not toy** — Personalized books get kept and re-read; toys get outgrown.
3. **Simple offer** — One book, $29.99, no subscriptions; character builder on Little Hero Labs site.
4. **Ages 1–7** — Works as first “my book” or for early readers; inner voice / courage theme.
5. **Who it’s for** — Birthdays, holidays, grandparents, new parents, new sibling, milestones.

### Content Variations
- **Occasion:** Birthday gift, holiday gift, “new big sibling” gift, grandparent gift.
- **Benefit:** “They see themselves,” “keepsake,” “one thoughtful gift instead of a pile of toys.”
- **Product:** Character builder, 16 pages, $29.99, no subs.

### Posting Schedule Suggestions
- Align with gift-giving seasons (holidays, back-to-school, spring birthdays).
- Evergreen: repost or refresh every 4–6 weeks with a new angle.
- Pair with other “personalized gifts for kids” or “gifts for grandparents” content.

### Analytics to Track
- Engagement (likes, comments, shares) per platform
- Clicks to blog post and to Little Hero Labs
- Saves (Instagram, Pinterest)
- Comments about personalized gifts or kids’ reactions
