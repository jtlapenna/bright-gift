## Cross-Platform Content Strategy

### Key Messaging
1. **Stocking Stuffer Focus:** Emphasize these are perfect for stocking stuffers
2. **Affordable Luxury:** Highlight that luxury doesn't mean expensive
3. **Quality Over Price:** Focus on thoughtful, quality gifts
4. **Versatile Gifting:** Perfect for multiple occasions (stockings, coworkers, just-because)
5. **Actionable:** Direct links to products make purchasing simple

### Content Variations
- **Category-Specific Posts:** Focus on one category (beauty, gourmet, cozy accessories)
- **Occasion-Focused Posts:** Highlight gifts for specific occasions (stockings, coworkers, secret Santa)
- **Budget-Focused Posts:** Emphasize the under $25 price point
- **Luxury Angle Posts:** Focus on how these feel expensive despite low prices

### Posting Schedule Suggestions
- **Holiday Seasons:** Increase frequency around major holidays (Christmas, Valentine's Day)
- **Gift Shopping Periods:** Post during peak gift shopping times (November-December)
- **Consistent Engagement:** Post 2-3 times per week with different angles
- **Evergreen Content:** Refresh and repost high-performing content monthly

### Analytics to Track
- Engagement rates per platform
- Click-through rates to blog post
- Comments about favorite stocking stuffers
- Shares and saves (especially Pinterest and Instagram)
- Time spent on blog post
- Conversion to actual gift purchases
