## Cross-Platform Content Strategy

### Key Messaging
1. **Milestone Celebration:** Emphasize that becoming a grandparent is a special milestone worth celebrating
2. **First-Time Focus:** Highlight gifts specifically for first-time grandparents
3. **Sentimental + Practical:** Focus on gifts that combine sentimentality with practicality
4. **Relationship Recognition:** Acknowledge the special grandparent-grandchild relationship
5. **Actionable:** Direct links to products make purchasing simple

### Content Variations
- **Category-Specific Posts:** Focus on one category (keepsakes, practical items, sentimental gifts)
- **Recipient-Focused Posts:** Highlight gifts for grandmas vs. grandpas
- **Occasion-Focused Posts:** Highlight gifts for specific occasions (baby showers, grandparent announcements)
- **Milestone Angle Posts:** Focus on celebrating the milestone of becoming a grandparent

### Posting Schedule Suggestions
- **Holiday Seasons:** Increase frequency around major holidays and family celebrations
- **Baby Shower Season:** Post during peak baby shower times (spring and summer)
- **Consistent Engagement:** Post 2-3 times per week with different angles
- **Evergreen Content:** Refresh and repost high-performing content monthly

### Analytics to Track
- Engagement rates per platform
- Click-through rates to blog post
- Comments about grandparent experiences
- Shares and saves (especially Pinterest and Instagram)
- Time spent on blog post
- Conversion to actual gift purchases
