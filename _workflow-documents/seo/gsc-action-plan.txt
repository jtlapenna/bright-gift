Google Search Console Action Plan
=================================

Use this workflow after a deploy that changes SEO-critical content or internal linking.

Rules before you start:
- Submit or inspect canonical URLs only.
- For blog posts, that means `https://bright-gift.com/blog/post-slug/`.
- Do not submit redirect variants such as `https://bright-gift.com/blog/post-slug`.
- Do not submit `.md` URLs.
- Do not submit paginated archive URLs such as `https://bright-gift.com/blog/?page=2`.

Immediate actions:

1. Submit the sitemap once:
   - `https://bright-gift.com/sitemap.xml`

2. Inspect and request indexing for the current refresh batch only:
   - `https://bright-gift.com/blog/gifts-under-25-for-coworkers/`
   - `https://bright-gift.com/blog/little-luxuries-under-25-mini-splurges-major-wow/`
   - `https://bright-gift.com/blog/clean-girl-2-0-minimalist-gifts-with-personality/`

3. If GSC shows `Page with redirect`:
   - Treat it as a source-tracing problem.
   - Find where the redirecting variant is still being emitted in code, scripts, docs, or operator inputs.
   - Remove that source before starting a new validation.

4. If a page is intentionally seasonal and off-season:
   - Leave the page live.
   - Do not submit it for indexing.
   - Do not add it back to the sitemap or reindex queue until it is seasonally refreshed.

Follow-through:
- Re-check the inspected URLs after Google recrawls them.
- Confirm the user-declared canonical matches the trailing-slash URL.
- Keep the request set small and deliberate; do not bulk-submit every URL on the site.
