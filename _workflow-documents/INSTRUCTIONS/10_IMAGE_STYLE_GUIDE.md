# BrightGift Image Style Guide

## Overview

This guide defines the visual aesthetic for all BrightGift blog images. The style combines modern flat illustration with subtle 3D/isometric depth, creating playful, dynamic compositions that feel inviting, optimistic, and sophisticated. Images should move beyond simple product assortments to convey concepts, emotions, and experiences related to thoughtful gift-giving.

---

## Core Aesthetic Principles

### 1. Overall Style & Mood

**High-Level Description:**
- Modern, clean, whimsical illustrative style with subtle 3D or isometric perspective
- Bright, optimistic, and sophisticated atmosphere
- Dynamic compositions that feel alive and engaging
- Playful yet refined—never childish or overly simplistic

**Mood Keywords:**
- Optimistic, joyful, creative, inviting, warm, sophisticated, whimsical, inspiring

**What to Avoid:**
- Static, flat lay-flat product photography
- Overly realistic or photorealistic rendering
- Harsh, saturated colors
- Rigid, grid-like arrangements
- Cluttered or busy compositions

---

## Visual Style Elements

### 2. Art Style & Technique

**Core Style:**
- **Modern Flat Illustration with Soft 3D/Isometric Elements**
  - Objects have clean, simplified, iconic appearance
  - Subtle depth and shading suggest three-dimensional form
  - Avoid hyper-detailed rendering or realistic textures

**Key Techniques:**
- **Rounded Edges:** All objects feature gently rounded corners and edges (never sharp)
- **Smooth, Matte Surfaces:** Objects have smooth, matte finishes with tactile yet refined look
- **Simplified Forms:** Focus on essential shapes, abstract unnecessary details
- **Clean Lines:** Crisp, clean vector-like lines for outlines and details
- **Soft Shading:** Use soft, diffused shading and internal gradients to suggest volume

**Stylization Level:**
- Objects should be immediately recognizable but rendered in simplified, artistic manner
- Balance between abstraction and recognition
- Maintain brand consistency while allowing creative interpretation

### 3. Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- **Teal/Mint Green:** `#00A99D` or `rgb(100, 200, 190)` to `rgb(150, 220, 210)`
- **Coral-Orange/Peach:** `#FF6B35` or `rgb(255, 160, 100)` to `rgb(255, 200, 120)`
- **Sunshine Yellow:** `#FFD700` or `rgb(255, 200, 120)`

**Supporting Colors:**
- Muted off-white, soft blues, subtle grays for objects
- Complementary accents used sparingly for emphasis

**Color Application:**
- **Gradient Backgrounds:** Smooth, subtle color transitions (e.g., teal fading into coral, or vice versa)
- **Split Backgrounds:** Large organic color zones transitioning smoothly
- **Soft Contrast:** Maintain harmonious contrast between elements and background
- **Vibrant but Soft:** Colors should feel energetic but never harsh or overwhelming

### 4. Lighting & Shadows

**Lighting Style:**
- **Soft, Diffused Lighting:** Gentle, ambient light without harsh spotlights
- **Even Distribution:** Warm and inviting atmosphere
- **Glow Effects:** Soft, luminous glows for light-emitting objects (lamps, screens, etc.)

**Shadow Treatment:**
- **Subtle, Soft Shadows:** Shadows are crucial for creating depth
- **Soft and Elongated:** Cast directly below or behind objects
- **Slightly Blurred:** Not sharply defined, complement background colors
- **Grounding Elements:** Shadows help objects feel grounded in the scene

### 5. Composition & Layout

**Perspective:**
- **Isometric or Dynamic Perspective:** Slightly elevated view allowing multiple objects to be viewed clearly
- **Varying Depths:** Position objects at different visual depths for spatial interest
- **Layered Elements:** Objects can overlap or recede into background naturally

**Arrangement Principles:**
- **Organic Flow:** Avoid rigid grids—arrange elements in flowing, natural manner
- **Balanced Asymmetry:** Visually balanced but not strictly symmetrical
- **Floating Elements:** Objects can appear to float or be suspended in space
- **Dynamic Movement:** Suggest gentle motion or interaction between elements

**Background Treatment:**
- **Gradient Backgrounds:** Smooth transitions between primary colors
- **Abstract Elements:** Small, scattered shapes (circles, crosses, squares, stars, dots) in complementary tones
- **Subtle Texture:** Abstract elements add texture without distracting from main subject
- **Negative Space:** Ample breathing room—avoid cluttered compositions

**Platform/Base (When Applicable):**
- Objects can be arranged on a defined platform (like a white-edged teal surface)
- Platform creates grounding while maintaining dynamic feel

### 6. Special Effects & Details

**Decorative Elements:**
- **Sparkles & Stars:** Subtle, star-shaped sparkles around key elements
- **Curved Lines/Arcs:** Gentle, curved lines connecting or highlighting elements
- **Abstract Connections:** Thin, winding lines (like cables or abstract connections) linking elements
- **Simple, Illustrative:** Decorative elements should be simple, not overly complex

**Texture:**
- **Minimal Textures:** Surfaces are generally matte
- **Very Subtle:** Almost imperceptible textures only if they enhance object identity
- **Never Distracting:** Textures should never compete with main subject

## Subject Matter & Creativity

### 7. Encouraging Creative Concepts

**Beyond Product Assortments:**
While product-focused images are sometimes necessary, prioritize creative concepts, scenes, and interactions over simple "lay-flat" arrangements of gift boxes.

**Conceptual Representation:**
Instead of just showing a gift, illustrate the *feeling* or *experience* of receiving or using the gift.

**Examples of Creative Approaches:**

**For Gift Guides:**
- **Instead of:** Flat-lay of gift boxes
- **Try:** Cozy scene where a mom is enjoying a thoughtful gift (luxurious blanket draped over a chair with a book and steaming mug, or personalized item gently held in hands, surrounded by soft light)

**For Tech Gifts:**
- **Instead of:** Just controllers and devices
- **Try:** Controllers connected by glowing lines, leading to a monitor displaying abstract, exciting visuals, with headphones suggesting immersive sound

**For Wellness Gifts:**
- **Instead of:** Just products on a surface
- **Try:** Serene spa setting with botanical elements, soft lighting, and objects suggesting relaxation and self-care

**For Experience Gifts:**
- **Instead of:** Generic travel items
- **Try:** Abstract representation of adventure or connection—floating elements suggesting movement, discovery, or shared moments

### 8. Human Elements (When Appropriate)

**Stylization:**
- Keep human elements stylized and consistent with overall flat/soft 3D illustration style
- Focus on hands, silhouettes, or abstract representations rather than detailed faces
- Maintain broad appeal and avoid overly specific representations

**Integration:**
- Human elements should feel natural and integrated, not forced
- Use to suggest interaction, use, or emotional connection
- Can be subtle—just a hand holding an object, or a silhouette in the background

### 9. Storytelling Through Imagery

**Each Image Should:**
- Subtly tell a story or evoke an emotion related to the blog post's theme
- Convey the essence of thoughtful gift-giving
- Suggest how a gift enhances someone's life, rather than just showing the gift itself
- Create a sense of possibility, connection, or delight

**Thematic Integration:**
- For specific blog posts, weave in thematic elements that resonate with the topic
- Wellness gifts: botanical elements, serene settings, soft lighting
- Tech gifts: subtle digital interfaces, futuristic touches, connection lines
- Holiday gifts: warm, cozy atmospheres, celebratory elements
- Personalized gifts: intimate, meaningful compositions

## Technical Specifications

### 10. Image Dimensions & Ratios

**Banner Images:**
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (wide horizontal)
- **Width:** 1200px minimum
- **Composition:** Wide, horizontal layouts with multiple elements or scenes

**OG (Open Graph) Images:**
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (wide horizontal)
- **Width:** 1200px minimum
- **Composition:** Focused, centered compositions with single strong focal point

### 11. Critical Requirements

**NEVER Include:**
- Text or typography of any kind
- Logos or watermarks
- Brand names or product labels visible in the image
- Realistic or photorealistic rendering
- Harsh shadows or dramatic lighting
- Cluttered or busy compositions

**ALWAYS Include:**
- Soft, rounded forms
- Gradient or split-color backgrounds
- Subtle shadows for depth
- Clean, balanced compositions
- Vibrant but soft pastel colors
- Dynamic, engaging arrangement

- `Clean lines`
- `Vector art style`
- `Matte surfaces`

**Color Keywords:**

- `Soft color transitions`

**Composition Keywords:**
- `Organic flow`
- `Balanced asymmetry`
- `Layered elements`
- `Abstract background elements`

**Mood Keywords:**

### 13. Prompt Structure Template

**For Banner Images:**
```
[Subject matter description with dynamic composition] arranged in a [flowing/organic/floating] manner with [specific elements] positioned at varying depths. [Background description with gradient/split colors]. [Special effects or decorative elements]. Modern flat illustration with soft 3D-style characters and objects, combined with subtle 2D decorative elements. Use warm, vibrant pastels (teal #00A99D, coral-orange #FF6B35, sunshine yellow #FFD700). Layout must be clean and giftable, using rounded forms, balanced negative space, and minimal visual clutter. The tone should feel cheerful, light, editorial, and creative — never realistic or photorealistic.
```

**For OG Images:**
```
Centered composition featuring [single focal element or small curated group] as the main subject, surrounded by [supporting elements] with [background treatment]. [Special effects or decorative elements]. Modern flat illustration with soft 3D-style characters and objects, combined with subtle 2D decorative elements. Use warm, vibrant pastels (teal #00A99D, coral-orange #FF6B35, sunshine yellow #FFD700). Layout must be clean and giftable, using rounded forms, balanced negative space, and minimal visual clutter. The tone should feel cheerful, light, editorial, and creative — never realistic or photorealistic.
```

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing any image prompt, verify:

- [ ] Style matches modern flat illustration with soft 3D elements
- [ ] Colors use teal, coral-orange, and sunshine yellow palette
- [ ] Composition is dynamic and engaging (not static lay-flat)
- [ ] Rounded edges and soft shadows included
- [ ] Gradient or split-color background specified
- [ ] Subject matter is creative (concept/scene, not just products)
- [ ] No text, logos, or watermarks mentioned
- [ ] Soft, diffused lighting specified
- [ ] Ample negative space for clean feel
- [ ] Style signature included at end of prompt
- [ ] Dimensions/aspect ratio specified (16:9, 1200px wide)

## Examples of Creative Subject Matter

**Instead of Simple Assortments, Try:**

1. **Cozy Scenes:** A luxurious blanket draped over a chair with a book, steaming mug, and soft lighting suggesting relaxation
2. **Abstract Concepts:** Floating elements connected by gentle lines suggesting connection, discovery, or adventure
3. **Lifestyle Moments:** Hands gently holding a personalized item, surrounded by soft light and meaningful details
4. **Thematic Environments:** Spa setting with botanical elements for wellness, tech setup with glowing connections for gaming
