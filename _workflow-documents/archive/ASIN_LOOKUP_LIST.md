## Search Tips for Finding ASINs

1. **Go to Amazon.com** and search for the exact product name
2. **Click on the product** to view the detail page
3. **Find the ASIN** in one of these places:
   - Product information section (scroll down on product page)
   - URL: `amazon.com/dp/ASIN-HERE` (ASIN is the 10-character code after `/dp/`)
   - Product Details section under "Technical Details"

4. **For Pre-Orders:** Make sure you're looking at the correct platform version (e.g., Switch 2 vs Switch)
