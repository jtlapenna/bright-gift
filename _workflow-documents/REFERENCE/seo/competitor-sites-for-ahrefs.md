## Top 10 Competitor Sites to Add to Ahrefs

Based on competitive research for Bright Gift, these are the 10 competitor sites that should be added to Ahrefs for monitoring and competitive analysis:

### 1. **giftlist.com**
- **Why:** Major AI-powered gift platform with extensive gift guides and strong SEO presence
- **Target Keywords:** Gift ideas, gift guides, AI-powered gifts, personalized gifts
- **Focus:** AI-powered gift recommendations and comprehensive gift guides
- **Type:** Direct AI competitor

### 2. **giftfinderai.co**
- **Why:** AI gift generator with Amazon integration and growing U.S. audience
- **Target Keywords:** AI gift finder, gift generator, personalized gift ideas
- **Focus:** AI-powered gift discovery with affiliate integration
- **Type:** Direct AI competitor

### 3. **giftruly.com**
- **Why:** Mobile-first AI gift discovery tool with Amazon links
- **Target Keywords:** Mobile gift finder, gift discovery, gift ideas
- **Focus:** Mobile-optimized AI gift discovery
- **Type:** Direct AI competitor

### 4. **giftgenius.io**
- **Why:** AI-driven Amazon affiliate gift platform, newer and less authoritative
- **Target Keywords:** Gift recommendations, AI gift ideas, Amazon gift links
- **Focus:** AI-powered gift suggestions with affiliate monetization
- **Type:** Direct AI competitor

### 5. **mygiftgenie.com**
- **Why:** Another AI gift suggestion engine often referenced in gift search articles
- **Target Keywords:** Gift genie, gift suggestions, AI gift recommendations
- **Focus:** AI-powered gift suggestion engine
- **Type:** Direct AI competitor

### 6. **mymap.ai**
- **Why:** Popular AI platform with a visual gift idea generator tool
- **Target Keywords:** Visual gift ideas, gift maps, AI gift generator
- **Focus:** Visual AI-powered gift idea generation
- **Type:** AI platform with gift tools

### 7. **giftsfinder.io**
- **Why:** AI and quiz-based gift tool targeting holidays and occasions
- **Target Keywords:** Gift finder, gift quiz, holiday gifts, occasion gifts
- **Focus:** Quiz-based AI gift discovery for holidays and occasions
- **Type:** Direct AI competitor

### 8. **giftlab.co**
- **Why:** Curated gift idea blog and product list site, ranking for "best gifts" and niches
- **Target Keywords:** Best gifts, curated gifts, gift lists, niche gifts
- **Focus:** Curated gift recommendations and blog content
- **Type:** Content competitor

### 9. **uncommongoods.com**
- **Why:** While not AI, ranks for high-volume gift terms with curated products
- **Target Keywords:** Unique gifts, unusual gifts, curated gifts, gift ideas
- **Focus:** Curated unique products and gift recommendations
- **Type:** E-commerce competitor (SEO benchmark)

### 10. **goodhousekeeping.com/gift-ideas/**
- **Why:** Not a competitor tool, but ranks for nearly all gift-related terms (SEO benchmark)
- **Target Keywords:** All major gift keywords (Christmas, Mother's Day, etc.)
- **Focus:** High-authority lifestyle content with gift guides
- **Type:** SEO benchmark (high-authority content site)

## How to Add These to Ahrefs

### Step 1: Access Ahrefs Competitive Research
1. Log into Ahrefs
2. Navigate to **Site Explorer**
3. Enter your domain: `bright-gift.com`
4. Click on **Organic Competitors** tab

### Step 2: Add Competitors Manually
1. Go to **Competing Domains** section
2. Click **Add Competitor**
3. Enter each competitor domain one by one

### Step 3: Use Content Gap Analysis
1. Go to **Content Gap** tool in Ahrefs
2. Enter your domain: `bright-gift.com`
3. Enter competitor domains (up to 5 at a time)
4. Review keywords they rank for but you don't

### Step 4: Set Up Competitor Tracking
1. Go to **Rank Tracker**
2. Select your project: "BrightGift SEO"
3. Navigate to **Competitor Comparison**
4. Add competitors to track their rankings
