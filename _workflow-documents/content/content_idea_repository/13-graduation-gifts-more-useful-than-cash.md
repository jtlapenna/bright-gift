# Graduation Gifts That Are More Useful Than Cash

**Priority:** HIGH  
**Status:** Not Started  
**Source:** May 2026 trending-topic research batch

## Why This Has High Potential

- **Active seasonal demand:** graduation gifting peaks in spring and early summer.
- **Data-backed hook:** NRF reported 36% of respondents planned to buy a 2025 graduation gift, with expected spending reaching $6.8 billion.
- **Differentiated angle:** cash is common, so the post can win by offering practical alternatives that feel more intentional.
- **BrightGift fit:** connects to existing graduation, tech, book, and practical-gift content.

## Recommended Slug

`graduation-gifts-that-are-more-useful-than-cash`

## Target Keywords

- **Primary:** graduation gifts
- **Secondary:** useful graduation gifts, gifts for graduates, high school graduation gifts, college graduation gifts
- **Long-tail:** graduation gifts more useful than cash, practical gifts for graduates, gifts for new college students

## Search Intent

Commercial gift-guide intent. Searchers may be considering cash but want something more memorable or practical.

## Content Angle

Position gifts as "cash-plus" or "cash alternatives" that support the graduate's next chapter: dorm life, first apartment, first job, travel, wellness, study setup, money management, and memory keeping.

## Suggested Structure

- Intro with graduation season and why cash is popular but not always the most personal.
- Quick picks by graduate path: college-bound, first job, trade school, moving out, gap year.
- Main list of 18-22 gifts.
- How to pair cash with a useful physical gift.
- Visible FAQ matching `faqSchema`.
- Related guides block and CTA to gift generator.
