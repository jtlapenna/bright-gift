## 🎨 WRITING STYLE (CRITICAL)

### DO:
- ✅ Write clear, simple, spartan sentences
- ✅ Use short, impactful sentences in active voice
- ✅ Direct address with "you" and "your"
- ✅ Bullet point lists where appropriate
- ✅ Friendly, conversational tone

### DON'T:
- ❌ Em dashes, semicolons
- ❌ Markdown formatting, asterisks, hashtags, emojis
- ❌ Metaphors, clichés, generalizations
- ❌ Filler words (can, may, just, very, really, literally, actually)
- ❌ Text on images

---

## 📊 SEO REQUIREMENTS

### Every Post Must Have:
1. **Primary keyword** in title (beginning preferred)
2. **Meta description** 150-160 characters with CTA
3. **H1** uses target keyword naturally (ONLY ONE H1)
4. **Word count** 2,000-2,500 words minimum
5. **Primary keyword** in first 100 words
6. **5-10 internal links** to related posts
7. **3-5 external links** to authoritative sources
8. **Image alt text** with primary keyword
9. **Canonical URL** set correctly
10. **No duplicate H1s** in content (layout provides H1)
