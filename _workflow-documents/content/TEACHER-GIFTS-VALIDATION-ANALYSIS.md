# Teacher Gifts Validation Analysis
**Date:** December 16, 2025  
**Purpose:** Validate that our "Best Gifts for Teachers" blog post list aligns with what teachers actually want

## Research Summary

Based on web searches including Reddit discussions, surveys, and teacher gift guides, here's what the research shows:

### What Teachers Actually Want (From Surveys & Sources)

1. **Gift Cards** - 75% of teachers prefer gift cards (especially Amazon)
2. **Handwritten Notes/Cards** - 51.5% of teachers cherish these
3. **Quality Pens/Markers** - Consistently mentioned as practical and appreciated
4. **Classroom Supplies** - Teachers spend their own money on these
5. **Self-Care Items** - Hand cream, spa items, relaxation products
6. **Personalized Items** - When personalized, items like mugs, tote bags, desk accessories are appreciated
7. **Insulated Tumblers** - Practical for keeping beverages at temperature
8. **Books for Classroom Library** - Supports their teaching environment
9. **Coffee/Tea Items** - Food and beverages are well-received
10. **Tote Bags** - Especially when personalized

### What Teachers DON'T Want

- **Generic Mugs** - Unless personalized (32.13% see as clutter)
- **Apple-Themed Items** - Overdone and cliché
- **Homemade Food** - 34.63% have concerns about food safety and dietary restrictions
- **Alcohol** - 38.88% feel uncomfortable receiving it
- **Trinkets/Knick-Knacks** - 32.13% view as clutter
- **Cheap Candles** - Weak scents, don't burn well
