# ASIN Extraction Guide for Gaming Gift Items

## How to Extract ASINs from amzn.to Links

### Quick Method:
1. Click on the amzn.to link (it redirects to Amazon)
2. Once on the Amazon product page, look at the URL
3. Find the ASIN in the URL format: `amazon.com/dp/BXXXXXXXXXX`
4. Copy the 10-character code (e.g., `B0F3VTFD6J`)

### Alternative Method:
1. Click on the amzn.to link to reach the Amazon product page
2. Scroll down to "Product Information" section
3. Find "ASIN" in the product details
4. Copy the ASIN code
