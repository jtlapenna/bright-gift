# SEO Recovery Strategy: From 100 Ahrefs Score to Google Visibility

- ✅ **Technical SEO**: 100/100 Ahrefs score (excellent)

## Root Cause: Google's "Crawl Budget Depletion"
When a site has had technical issues for months, Google reduces its crawl frequency and indexing priority. Even after fixes, it takes time to rebuild trust.
